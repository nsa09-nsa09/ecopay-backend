1. GENERAL PROVISIONS

1.1. This Consent and Privacy Policy (the "Policy") sets out how the personal data of EcoPay Users (the "Service") is collected, processed, stored, used, transferred and protected.

1.2. The personal data operator is [full legal entity name], BIN [___], address: [___] (the "Operator", "EcoPay", "we").

1.3. Personal data is processed in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan No. 94-V of 21 May 2013 "On Personal Data and Their Protection" and other applicable regulations.

1.4. By ticking the consent checkbox at registration, you grant the Operator consent to process your personal data under this Policy. Consent is voluntary; you may withdraw it as described in Section 10.


2. LEGAL BASES FOR PROCESSING

2.1. Personal data is processed on the following bases:
— the consent of the data subject (you);
— the need to perform the contract (the User Agreement) to which you are a party;
— compliance with the Operator's statutory obligations (including financial and tax accounting and fraud prevention);
— protection of the legitimate interests of the Operator and third parties that does not infringe your rights.


3. WHAT DATA WE PROCESS

3.1. Account data: email address, display name, encrypted (hashed) password, chosen avatar, role, account status.

3.2. Telecom Room data: phone number and/or other connection identifiers you provide to organise tariff sharing. Such data is stored in encrypted form and disclosed to the Room Owner only after successful payment and only to the extent necessary to grant access.

3.3. Payment data: transaction information (amounts, statuses, transaction identifiers). Full bank card details are processed by the licensed Payment Provider; the Operator does not store card numbers or security codes.

3.4. Technical data: IP address, device and browser information (user-agent), event logs, sign-in and activity data, cookies and similar technologies.

3.5. Reputation and interaction data: reviews, ratings, support tickets, dispute information.

3.6. Geolocation data: processed only with your separate consent to display approximate operator signal quality; such data is indicative only.


4. PURPOSES OF PROCESSING

4.1. Personal data is processed in order to:
— register and authenticate the User and provide access to the Service;
— organise Rooms, match Owners and Members, and grant and confirm access;
— process payments, payouts and refunds and maintain financial records;
— perform verification, prevent fraud and assess risk;
— handle tickets and disputes and provide support;
— send notifications related to use of the Service;
— comply with legal requirements;
— improve the quality and security of the Service.


5. DISCLOSURE TO THIRD PARTIES

5.1. Personal data may be disclosed to:
— the Payment Provider — to process payments and payouts;
— another Room participant (Owner or Member) — solely to the extent necessary to grant/receive access (e.g. a telecom identifier to the Owner after payment);
— infrastructure providers (hosting, cloud services, notification tools) acting on the Operator's behalf and bound to maintain confidentiality;
— government authorities — in the cases and manner provided by the laws of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

5.2. The Operator does not sell personal data to third parties and does not transfer it for advertising purposes without your consent.


6. CROSS-BORDER TRANSFER

6.1. Where data is processed using infrastructure outside the Republic of Kazakhstan, the Operator ensures transfers only to countries that guarantee an adequate level of protection, or on the basis of your consent and with the necessary safeguards, in accordance with the law.


7. RETENTION PERIODS

7.1. Personal data is retained for as long as necessary to achieve the purposes of processing and for the periods set by law, in particular:
— audit logs — at least 180 days;
— payment data — at least 3 years;
— support tickets — at least 1 year.

7.2. Upon account deletion, personal data is anonymised; financial and audit records are retained for the required periods in de-identified form or to the extent required by law.


8. SECURITY MEASURES

8.1. The Operator applies legal, organisational and technical measures to protect personal data, including: encryption of data in transit (TLS) and encryption of sensitive data at rest, access-rights segregation, immutable action logs, restricted access to identifiers, protection against unauthorised access, and regular backups.


9. RIGHTS OF THE DATA SUBJECT

9.1. You have the right to:
— obtain information about the processing of your personal data;
— request clarification, amendment or supplementation of the data;
— request blocking or deletion (de-identification) of data processed in breach of the law;
— withdraw previously given consent;
— appeal the Operator's actions to the authorised data-protection body and to the courts.

9.2. To exercise your rights, send a request to the email address in Section 11.


10. WITHDRAWAL OF CONSENT

10.1. You may withdraw your consent to personal data processing at any time by contacting the Operator. After withdrawal, the Operator ceases processing, except where processing is necessary to perform statutory obligations or to protect rights and legitimate interests.

10.2. Withdrawal of consent may make further use of the Service impossible, as the processing of certain data is necessary for it to function.


11. COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

11.1. The Service uses cookies and similar technologies for operation, security, authentication and to improve the user experience. You can manage cookies in your browser settings; disabling certain cookies may limit functionality.


12. CHILDREN

12.1. The Service is not intended for persons under 18. The Operator does not knowingly collect minors' data. If such data is identified, it is deleted.


13. CHANGES TO THE POLICY

13.1. The Operator may amend this Policy. The current version is published in the Service with the update date. Material changes may be additionally communicated.
